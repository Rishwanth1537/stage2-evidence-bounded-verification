## 1. What the project is
An MCP (Model Context Protocol) server and proxy (`server/`) that checks an AI agent's planned tool call before it runs: schema validation, text grounding, and argument-level grounding with a conformal threshold. The original claim, that a cheap deterministic checker catches hallucinated tool-call arguments as well as an LLM judge, was disproved by our own experiments (section 4). The project is now a **study of what evidence decides whether a tool-call argument is wrong, and of asking the user instead of blocking** (section 3).

## 2. Why the direction changed
The professor asked: "if modern LLMs can beat this, there is no use of it." We measured it. On natural (model-made) errors in DeRef every LLM judge we ran beats the deterministic context checker; on errors we injected ourselves they tie; on the human-attributed AgentHallu slice (CONFIRMATORY) the checker is as good as or better than the judges because those errors are mostly invented text (section 4, row 12). So the honest claim is about *which verifier suits which kind of error*, and about the information a verifier is given.

## 3. Current thesis and scope (decided by the user, 2026-10-30; main-track-capable scope)
> Verifying a tool-call argument is limited by the evidence the verifier can see, not by its size. A verifier can only decide the errors its evidence decides; for the rest the right action is to **ask the user**, with disagreement between models as the trigger and a distribution-free budget on how often it interrupts.

Contributions C1 evidence ladder and decidability map; C2 information-not-scale ablation; C3 ask-policy with an interruption budget; C4 negative results (state lookup, injection); C5 artefacts. Hypotheses and kill rules: `PAPER_PLAN_V2.md` section 4 and `PREREGISTRATION.md` (Deviations 12, 13, 14; Amendment 12b). Venue plan: ICLR 2027 workshop (suggested deadline 1 Feb 2027) first, EMNLP/NeurIPS 2027 for the full version (`PAPER_PLAN_V2.md` section 10).

## 5. What is lacking (honest list)
1. **No locked test.** Everything is on data we inspected repeatedly; BFCL is fully explored; AgentHallu is the only confirmatory slice (31 positives). The test halves of fcrb/cfb are untouched (ledger: `benchmarks/test_run_ledger.jsonl`).
2. **No human audit yet.** The user must fill `review/audit2/audit_sample_blind.csv` (98 items; not the old `review/audit/`). Until then every claim built on benchmark labels is conditional.
3. **Judge conditions are uneven**: Claude judges run as subagents (batches of 25, no temperature control); Groq and NVIDIA judges at temperature 0. Never pooled. A main-track table needs at least three API providers (keys missing: Anthropic API, OpenRouter).
4. **Real self-consistency is untested** (k samples from one model as the ask trigger; peer agreement needs 29 extra model calls). The script is specified in the playbook, not written.
5. **Narrow data**: BFCL-derived data dominate; MCP-Atlas and tau2 data have no natural errors; AgentDojo labels are heuristic; no live-MCP evidence.
6. **Guarantee is a sketch** (marginal and cluster-bootstrap rules; no proof write-up; shift study not done).
7. **Written material is behind**: deck, speaker guide and parts of README still carry the old claim (deliberately untouched on the user's instruction).
8. **Committed but not merged**: branch `stage2-evidence-bounded-verification` (pushed to origin; three commits c019c62, 058fd19, 77a789c on top of 886c98c; review-eval-updates is unchanged at 886c98c); audit key files and generated data are deliberately not in git.

## 6. Why it is not (yet) novel, and why results may not hold
Every ingredient exists: argument-level judges and derivation paths (Ma et al. 2610.18497), live-state checks (StateMirror 2610.32648), schema/registry checks (Closed-World Resolution 2609.19425), policy gates (NOMOS 2610.11030), executability evaluation (Prefix Executability 2610.40444), conformal guarantees for verifiers. The candidate contributions are the **measurement** (decidability map across evidence sources, information-vs-scale with a controlled ladder, complementarity of context and judge verifiers) and the **ask-policy with a calibrated interruption budget** (scout rated about 70% open: `scout/SCOUT_5_new_ideas.md`). Results may not hold because: dev-only and repeatedly inspected; benchmark labels partly circular or noisy (section 4 row 11); small samples (43 strong BFCL wrong calls, 31 AgentHallu positives); our injected errors flatter the deterministic checker; agent-mode judges are batched; model versions drift.
